Subject: Update to Remote Work Policy — Effective March 1, 2026

Dear Colleagues,

Following the leadership team's review of our current hybrid work
arrangements, we are pleased to announce an updated Remote Work
Policy effective March 1, 2026.

Key changes:
  1. Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week (previously 2).
  2. Core collaboration hours are now 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM KST on
     all working days, regardless of work location.
  3. All remote workdays must be registered in the HR portal by
     5:00 PM on the preceding Friday.
  4. Employees in their first 6 months of employment remain subject
     to the previous 1-day remote allowance.

The full updated policy document is available on the HR portal
under Policies > Work Arrangements > Remote Work Policy v3.2.

Please direct any questions to your HR business partner or to
hr-questions@yourcompany.com.

Human Resources Department
